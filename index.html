Privacy Policy — Status Downloader

Last updated: October 2026

Status Downloader ("the app") is developed as an independent personal project. This page explains what information the app accesses and how it is used.

What the app accesses

The app reads WhatsApp's status cache folder on your device, but only after you explicitly grant access through Android's system folder picker. The app cannot and does not access any other folder, your messages, contacts, or any other app's data.

When you choose to save a status, the app copies that file into its own folder in your device's gallery (Pictures/StatusSaver or Movies/StatusSaver). No files are uploaded anywhere — everything stays on your device.

Data we do not collect

The app has no account system, no login, and does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information, messages, contacts, or media to the developer or any third party.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob (a Google service) to support free use of the app. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other technical data to serve and measure ads. This data collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can review and reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Settings app (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Permissions used

  • Storage access (via folder picker): to read WhatsApp's status files you've chosen to grant access to.
  • Storage write (older Android versions only): to save a copy of a status to your gallery.
  • Internet / network state: required by the AdMob SDK to load and display ads.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
